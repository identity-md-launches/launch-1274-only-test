Local adversarial review of `src/TESTToken.sol` and its compiled runtime:

- **Authority:** the only state-changing entry points are `approve`, `transfer` and `transferFrom`. Approval writes the caller's allowance. Delegated transfers require that holder's allowance, even if called by the deployer. There is no fallback, initializer, role, owner, pause, freeze, blacklist, fee setter or rescue function. Tests probe administrative selectors from the deployer and a stranger, then confirm the holder remains able to transfer.
- **Supply:** the constructor assigns 10^27 units to the deployer exactly once. `totalSupply` is a compile-time constant. No callable path mints or burns, and zero-address transfers revert. Ordinary transfers to the supplied dead-address remainder recipient do not reduce supply.
- **Value conservation:** transfer debits and credits are equal. The recipient balance is read after subtraction, preserving self-transfer balances. A finite allowance is reduced before transfer; any later failure reverts the whole call, including the allowance. The unchecked arithmetic is limited to subtraction after balance/allowance bounds and recipient addition, which is bounded by the fixed aggregate supply. Tests cover maximum inputs, zero, self transfers, exact and infinite allowances, revocation and failed operations.
- **Running totals:** the actual token holder is credited with each successful outgoing amount. Transfers back do not erase prior totals; repeated self-transfers may produce a total greater than supply. Running-total addition uses checked arithmetic. A theoretical uint256 overflow would revert the transfer; reaching it would require more than 10^50 full-supply transfers by that sender. Events and stored totals are checked against an independent model across randomized sequences.
- **External execution:** the token makes no external calls, so it has no token callback, oracle, router or reentrancy dependency. The compiled runtime is scanned for the forbidden destructive and delegating opcodes while skipping PUSH data. There is no proxy or linked library.
- **Launch and trading:** the factory initially holds the entire supply. The token neither identifies privileged launch participants nor sends the swarm allocation itself. A real local v4 PoolManager proves exact settlement during single-sided seeding, buys and sells in both currency orders. A separate two-leaf test distributor models whole-value claims; integer-liquidity dust is accounted for at the requested remainder recipient.

No concrete defect was found in the reviewed token paths. This is a local review, not an independent audit. It does not verify a deployed factory, the real MerkleDistributor, production pool initialization hooks, or current IMD behavior. The optional mainnet fork test was skipped without an RPC URL. The external protected verifier's environment and policy contracts are outside this repository.
